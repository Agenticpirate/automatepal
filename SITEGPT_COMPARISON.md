# AutomatePal versus SiteGPT - internal review, October 29, 2026

AutomatePal is a narrower fictional-data preview. It does not currently offer better overall customer service than SiteGPT. It demonstrates a useful service-management and sandbox-billing workflow, but breadth, real human delivery, operational assurance and independent answer-quality evidence trail the documented incumbent. No comparative accuracy, satisfaction, response-time or reliability benchmark has been run. Vendor assertions are not independent measurements.

## Top three gaps for Sunday November 1 internal gate

1. **Repeatable customer-answer evidence.** Run versioned fictional retrieval+guidance+multilingual+injection+style fixtures end to end with stage diagnostics, twice on a pinned release. Include all misses/fallbacks, distinguish safe rejection from useful answer, fix regressions. Gate: no cross-tenant leakage/unsupported-payment answer; publish measured counts, not a zero-error or competitor-beating number. Free provider quota/access may constrain model runs; do not extract a hosted key.
2. **Independent isolation and recovery proof.** Populated independent second-user browser workflow plus scoped API/denial checks; source-only separate config/DB setup; manual backup to an approved private destination and isolated restore rehearsal. Gate: reproducible sign-in/source/answer/delete path and recovered fixture counts. Currently SQL rollback fixtures and clean install do not satisfy this.
3. **Safe access and honest operational contract.** Keep judge route owner-only until explicit approval, address Gemini free-tier region/age restrictions, finish approved legal draft review and a measured recorded sandbox flow. State notification/human reply transport is deferred, no uptime/response SLA. Do not promise production support while critical vendor/retention/BAA boundaries remain open.

These priorities beat chasing channel counts before the gate. Customer-visible replies/notifications, website sync and teams are the largest product breadth gaps, but not approved shortcuts around the current no-transport/free-only limits. Roadmap them explicitly; only implement after scoped approval and tests. No production launch or public submission approval is implied.
