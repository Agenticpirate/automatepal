# Security policy

AutomatePal is an experimental beta (`automatepal.onrender.com`), built for the PayPal AI Hackathon 2026 with fictional data only. It is not a production service.

## Reporting

Email raviteja.journal@gmail.com with a subject line starting `[security]`. Include the affected page or demo-link URL, what you observed, and when. Do not include secrets, credentials or other people's personal data in the report.

Reports are reviewed manually by one maintainer. No response time or fix timeline is promised. Good-faith research reported this way will not be treated as hostile.

## Scope notes

- Tenant isolation, quotas and guardrails are documented in HARDENING.md and PROOF.md with their measured evidence.
- Known limits (free-tier hosting, no SLA, manual recovery) are listed in OPERATIONS.md and on the public status page.
- Please do not test against real customer data; none should exist in this system.
