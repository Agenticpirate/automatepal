## Early-review readiness gate

Do not submit merely to meet the early date. All assets below need inspection and Ravi's approval of the actual public submission and audiences.

- [ ] Meaningful PayPal sandbox integration central to the demonstrated problem, together with meaningful AI functionality. Public source says both are required. Current support/knowledge AI and fictional billing display alone must not be assumed sufficient. Assess actual existing sandbox/API path and prove its role with no payment execution or spend. If that cannot be shown within current authority, mark eligibility risk and ask main rather than turn on payments.
- [ ] Working hosted demo or complete tested repository setup/run instructions. Static marketing specimen does not satisfy the functional demo gate. Judge entry path must not depend on expired one-hour links; explain Google sign-in, fictional seed data, free-tier startup, provider access, quotas and safe limitations. Never publish credentials.
- [ ] Public open-source repository with all required source/assets/run instructions and visible root license. Root LICENSE exists, but public visibility, license recognition and clean-room setup must be checked live before submission.
- [ ] Text description: audience, concrete problem, implemented features, tested flow and limits. No production/parity/SLA/zero-error claims. List model/AI service and other tools plus how each is used. Do not list unused sponsor products.
- [ ] Public YouTube demo strictly under3 minutes showing functioning product on its intended device. No unapproved third-party trademarks/music/material. Prepare a rights-safe recording/script, inspect final video, measure duration, confirm public visibility and actual URL. Video upload/publication requires Ravi approval.
- [ ] Evidence pack: exact release/payment flag, full regression results, measured AI successes and retained failures, tenant isolation scope, desktop/mobile inspected pixels, known gaps, rollback note. Do not present synthetic tests as independent real-user acceptance.
- [ ] Owner review: exact submission fields, repository/demo/video links, screenshots, privacy/rights check and permission to submit. Main owns approval and submission coordination. No submit action is authorized by this plan.
